--- Snubber 损耗公式 ---
关断: E_off = 0.5×C×((Vout+Vf)² - (IL_peak×Rdson)²)
开通: E_on  = 0.5×C×((Vout+Vf)² - (IL_valley×Rdson)²)
P_snub = (E_on + E_off) × fsw  (需迭代后计算)
